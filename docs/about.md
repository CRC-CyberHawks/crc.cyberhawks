# About CRC Cyber Hawks

**Detect • Defend • Dominate.**  
We are the official student-led cybersecurity organization at **Cosumnes River College**, dedicated to building practical, hands-on offensive and defensive security skills outside the classroom.

## Our Background & Mission
Founded to bridge the gap between textbook theory and real-world security operations, the CRC Cyber Hawks prepare students for regional and national collegiate competitions, industry certifications, and cybersecurity careers. No prior experience is required—we train members from their first Linux command all the way to live incident response.

## What We Focus On
* **Defensive Operations (Blue Team):** Hardening Linux and Windows servers, analyzing network traffic, and responding to simulated live cyberattacks in collegiate defense competitions.
* **Capture The Flag (CTF):** Solving timed challenges in cryptography, web exploitation, digital forensics, and OSINT.
* **Career & Skill Building:** Resume reviews, home lab walkthroughs, and certification study groups (Security+, Network+, CySA+).
